# A little less on your mind.

Knotlet is a home for your memories. Gather the things worth keeping from emails, notes, and AI conversations. Bring them into notebooks, or just ask when you need a detail.

- **Gather your memories.** Save a note, forward an email, or bring something useful from an AI conversation.
- **Make room to think.** Bring memories into a notebook. Write, sketch, and work through a plan.
- **Just ask Knotlet.** Find a detail without remembering where you put it.

[Explore Knotlet](https://knotlet.app) · [Open your workspace](https://knotlet.app/home)

Knotlet is currently private. Public sign-ups are not available yet.
